\section{Right Conoid Geometry}

\subsection{Definition and Metric}

The infinite right conoid is parametrized by
\[
\mathbf{r}(u,v) = \bigl( v \cos u,\ v \sin u,\ h(u) \bigr).
\]

For the recommended working model (right helicoid, \( h(u) = c u \)):
\begin{equation}
ds^2 = (v^2 + c^2)\, du^2 + dv^2.
\end{equation}

\subsection{Curvature}

Gaussian curvature:
\begin{equation}
K = -\frac{c^2}{(v^2 + c^2)^2} < 0.
\end{equation}

Mean curvature: \( H = 0 \) (minimal surface).

\subsection{Orthonormal Coframe and Volume Form}

\[
e^1 = \sqrt{v^2 + c^2}\, du, \quad e^2 = dv,
\qquad
\mathrm{vol} = \sqrt{v^2 + c^2}\, du \wedge dv.
\]

\subsection{Dirac Operator (Schematic)}

The Dirac operator on the right helicoid takes the form
\[
D = i \gamma^1 \left( \frac{1}{\sqrt{v^2 + c^2}} \partial_u + \cdots \right) 
  + i \gamma^2 \partial_v + \text{connection terms}.
\]

\begin{remark}
Due to the non-compact nature of the surface and linear growth of the area element along the rulings, the spectrum of \(D\) is continuous. Dual-Zero hyperreal regularization is required to obtain a well-defined spectral action.
\end{remark}

\section{Dual-Zero Hyperreal Structure}

\subsection{Definition}

\begin{definition}[Dual-Zero Hyperreal Structure]
A Dual-Zero structure on the hyperreals consists of a pair of signed infinitesimals \(\varepsilon, \delta\) equipped with a duality involution \(*\) satisfying \(\varepsilon^* = \delta\) and \(\delta^* = \varepsilon\).
\end{definition}

\subsection{Proposed Scaling with Parameters}

\begin{equation}
\varepsilon = \ell_0 \exp(-1/\omega_0), \qquad 
\delta = -\ell_0 \exp(-\omega_0).
\end{equation}

\subsection{Claimed Physical Relations}

Newton’s constant (claimed):
\begin{equation}
G_N = \frac{64\pi \ell_0^2}{45}.
\end{equation}

Other claimed results from the spectral action include a Higgs mass near 126 GeV and three fermion generations.

\section{Binary-Icosahedral Bridges}

\subsection{Definition of the Binary Icosahedral Group}

\begin{definition}[Binary Icosahedral Group]
The \textbf{binary icosahedral group} \(2I\) is the central extension
\[
1 \to \{\pm 1\} \to 2I \to A_5 \to 1
\]
of order 120, realized as a discrete subgroup of \(\mathrm{SU}(2)\).
\end{definition}

\subsection{Definition of a Bridge}

\begin{definition}[Binary-Icosahedral Bridge]
A binary-icosahedral bridge is a structure morphism that incorporates representations of \(2I\) into the spectral triple on the right conoid, weighted by the Dual-Zero infinitesimals. The model uses exactly **twelve** such bridges.
\end{definition}

\subsection{Proposed Explicit Construction of the Twelve Bridges}

We propose the following explicit construction:

Let the twelve bridges be located at equally spaced angles
\[
\theta_k = \frac{k \pi}{6}, \quad k = 0,1,\dots,11.
\]

For each bridge \(k\), define
\begin{equation}
B_k = \varepsilon \cdot \rho_k(g) + \delta \cdot \rho_k(g)^*,
\end{equation}
where \(\rho_k\) is a 2-dimensional representation of \(2I\) assigned to the \(k\)-th sector, and \(g \in 2I\) is chosen according to the conjugacy class associated with angle \(\theta_k\).

The total bridge contribution to the Dirac operator is
\begin{equation}
D_{\text{bridge}} = \sum_{k=0}^{11} B_k \otimes \chi_k(v),
\end{equation}
where \(\chi_k(v)\) is a localized cutoff function (e.g., Gaussian) supported near the ruling at angle \(\theta_k\).

This construction distributes the twelve bridges symmetrically around the axis of the conoid and weights each by the Dual-Zero pair \((\varepsilon, \delta)\).

\begin{remark}
This is a proposed explicit construction. The following details still require precise definition:
\begin{itemize}
    \item Assignment of specific representations \(\rho_k\) to each bridge.
    \item Explicit form of the cutoff functions \(\chi_k(v)\).
    \item Verification that \(D_{\text{bridge}}\) preserves self-adjointness and compatibility with the real structure.
\end{itemize}
\end{remark}

\subsection{Connection to Three Generations}

The signed Dual-Zero action on representations of \(2I\), combined with the twelve bridges, is proposed to generate **exactly three chiral fermion generations** through the duality involution and representation branching.
