## 2. Geometric Cabibbo angle

$$
\theta_{12} \approx \eta_{12} \times \frac{\pi}{12} \approx 0.8607 \times 15^\circ \approx 12.91^\circ
$$

**Realization**
- Extracted as an `A_F` left rotation acting primarily on the down-type doublets.
- Quaternionic summand `ℍ` supplies an enhancement relative to the complex summand `ℂ` that governs the up sector.
- In the basis where `Y_u` is nearly diagonal, the CKM matrix is essentially the down-sector left diagonalizer.
- PDG light-first ordering of singular vectors is required for correct angle reading (heavy-first SVD ordering previously swapped Cabibbo into the 2–3 block).

## 3. Quark mass ratios

Generated by radial localization (Casimir potentials) + Dual-Zero regulator, with overall scales fixed from `ℓ₀` and the spectral action.

**Status after hardening**
- Charm / strange / down ratios: good agreement with experiment.
- Up-quark mass: residual factor ~2 tension (still light but not exact).
- Top mass: used as the `ℓ₀` anchor (exact by construction).

Explicit numerical Yukawa matrices after the `κ_d` / `A_F` CG refinement were obtained by bi-unitary diagonalization of the geometrically generated `Y_u`, `Y_d`. The dominant improvement relative to earlier versions was the correct relative left rotation between up and down sectors; no angle-targeted fitting was used.
