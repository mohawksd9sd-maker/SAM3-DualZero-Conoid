## Plain-language summary (for Shawn)

- **Verdict: none of these structures fixes the top or the Higgs.** The best each can do is reproduce the doc 60 result: **M_t = 190.36 GeV, M_h = 180.50 GeV**.
- **Cube flavour groups** (A₄, S₄, O_h, Δ(27), T′, and the "seven" group T₇). For every one I built the matrices and found every allowed Yukawa pattern.
  - **Patterns with no free parameters** make all three generations as heavy as the top (Y ∝ 1), or make two of them equally heavy. That would put the charm quark at the top mass, which is ruled out by a factor of about 300.
  - **Patterns with free parameters** have their parameters fitted to the measured quark masses. That makes the light-generation share about 7×10⁻⁶, so the top stays at 190.4.
  - **Why the top can't move.** Lowering the top to 172.6 would need the two light generations to carry 55% of the top's Yukawa strength. The measured masses forbid that.
- **A₄ does predict neutrino mixing**, the famous tribimaximal pattern: sin²θ₁₂ = 1/3, sin²θ₂₃ = 1/2, θ₁₃ = 0. This is a genuine parameter-free prediction, and it is **ruled out**:
  - θ₁₃ is measured to be non-zero, so the miss is **39σ**;
  - θ₁₂ is 2.2σ off;
  - inside Pati–Salam the same symmetry makes the quark mixing matrix the identity (V_us = 0 instead of 0.225).
- **The cube as the internal space** (a 3-torus on the cubic lattice and its orbifolds):
  - Plain or rotated cubes give matched left/right pairs (no chirality), and full copies do not change the top.
  - The full octahedral twist kills every constant mode.
  - Chirality appears only with a magnetic flux on one face plus a reflection. The flux number sets the generations, so 3 is put in by hand. The square-lattice Yukawas it produces give charm/top ≥ 0.12 (measured ≈ 0.003), and M_t lands between 160 and 189 GeV depending on the Higgs direction, never on a data-consistent texture.
  - The cube's Kaluza–Klein towers come in shells of 6, 12, 8. Any of them below the unification scale moves 1/α by 0.6–2.3 and spoils the doc 55/60 unification.
- **Notes.**
  - **Ezekiel:** a wheel within a wheel read as nested tori (torus fibrations) was already covered by K3 in doc 62. Nothing new is forced, so this is not testable beyond that.
  - **Ark ratios:** 13 ratios and roots were tried, and none lands in the band the top needs (expected by chance: 0.25). Reading W/L = 1/6 as y_t²/4g² gives M_t = 174.0 but M_h = 153.4. That is post-hoc and fails the Higgs.
  - **Seven lampstands:** the concrete versions are T₇ = ℤ₇⋊ℤ₃ (included above, same verdict) and the Fano-plane/octonion Yukawa. The octonion Yukawa gives three degenerate pairs and one massless state, so it is ruled out. G₂ manifolds have no explicit metric to compute with, so they are not testable.

## 1. Discrete flavour symmetries of the cube

### 1.1 Method

The groups are generated by breadth-first closure over explicit generator matrices in all the irreps used (`GROUPS`), with orders checked:

| Group | Order |
|---|---|
| A₄ | 12 |
| S₄ | 24 |
| O_h | 48 |
| Δ(27) | 27 |
| T′ | 24 |
| T₇ | 21 |

The invariant Yukawa couplings \(Y_{ij}{}^{a}\) for \(\bar L\otimes R\otimes H\) are the image of the group-averaged projector. For each assignment I sampled 300 random invariant couplings and Higgs/flavon directions, both random and aligned at (1,0,0) and (1,1,1). For each sample I recorded the normalized singular values \(\sigma_i/\sigma_1\) and the light share \(\sum_{i\ge2}\sigma_i^2/\sigma_1^2\), the quantity that enters \(a=3+n+x\) with \(x=(3+n)\cdot\text{share}\).

Two patterns cover every row:

- **Parameter-free textures are degenerate.** A triplet-triplet-singlet coupling gives Y ∝ 1. A triplet Higgs in S₄/O_h gives two equal masses and one zero. In both cases m_c/m_t = 1, while the data say ≈ 3×10⁻³ at Λ.
- **The other textures are free.** Their 2–3 complex parameters (plus flavon VEVs) must be fitted to the six quark masses. After the fit the light share is the data value, ≈ 7×10⁻⁶.

**Top/Higgs through the two-loop pipeline** (`pipeline`, the doc 60 machinery with the doc 63 accounting, \(\Delta N=0\)):

| rank-2 degenerate (S₄/O_h/T′, triplet H) | 1 | 1 | 160.11 | 133.51 | m_c = m_t: excluded |

The top would need share = 0.55, which lies between these rows. No assignment produces it without free parameters, and any texture with share 0.55 has m_c/m_t ~ 0.7.

### 1.3 A₄ neutrino mixing (Altarelli–Feruglio type, `a4_mixing`)

The setup:

- L ~ 3 and e^c, μ^c, τ^c ~ 1, 1″, 1′;
- φ_T is aligned along the T-invariant direction, which makes the charged leptons diagonal (checked numerically);
- the effective Majorana mass is \((LL)_1\,\xi + (LL)_{3_s}\cdot\phi_S\), with φ_S along the S-invariant direction.

Over 50 random complex couplings:

| | Prediction | Spread over couplings | NuFIT 6.0 (NO) | Pull |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| sin²θ₁₂ | 1/3 | < 10⁻¹² | 0.307 ± 0.012 | +2.2σ |
| sin²θ₁₃ | 0 | < 10⁻²⁷ | 0.02215 ± 0.00057 | **−38.9σ** |
| sin²θ₂₃ | 1/2 | < 10⁻¹³ | 0.470 ± 0.015 | +2.0σ |

- **Parameter count, lepton sector:** 3 charged-lepton Yukawas and 2 complex neutrino couplings fit the masses. The 3 angles are predictions. The vacuum alignment needs a driving-field sector, which is extra and not counted.
- **Verdict:** a true parameter-free prediction, and it is excluded by θ₁₃. Getting θ₁₃ ≈ 8.6° requires extra breaking terms with new parameters, which is a fit, not a prediction.
- **Pati–Salam consequence:** quarks and leptons share the flavour assignment, so Y_u and Y_d are both diagonal in the T basis and V_CKM = 1 (V_us = 0 against 0.2250). This is excluded.

## 2. The cube as internal space

The setup is 7D: \(\Gamma^{\mu}=\gamma^\mu\otimes 1\) and \(\Gamma^{4+a}=\gamma_5\otimes\sigma_a\) (`seven_d_zero_modes`).

| Internal space | Zero modes (L, R) per 7D spinor | Chiral? |
|---|---|---|
| T³ (cubic lattice), constant modes | (4, 4) | no |
| T³/ℤ₂ rotation, twist ±1 | (2, 2) | no |
| T²×S¹/ℤ₂ reflection, no flux | (2, 2) | no |
| T²×S¹/ℤ₂ reflection, M flux units on T² | (2M, 0) or (0, 2M) | **yes** |
| T³/O (binary octahedral 2O on the spinor) | 0 invariant constant modes | — |

- Rotations commute with γ₅, so every rotation orbifold of the cube is vector-like. Its modes are full copies of H_F, and by doc 63 full copies leave y_t invariant (M_t = 190.36).
- The full octahedral group acts irreducibly on the internal spinor, so nothing survives.
- **Magnetized version.** Chirality needs flux plus a reflection. The generation number equals the flux M (chosen, not forced). The Yukawas are triple overlaps of the square-lattice (τ = i) theta functions (`square_texture`, doc 62 machinery):
  - the light share is ≥ 0.030 for every Higgs direction, with σ₂/σ₁ ≥ 0.12 against data ≈ 0.003;
  - M_t ranges over **160.1 – 189.2 GeV** and M_h over 133.5 – 177.6 GeV, depending on the Higgs direction (1 discrete choice);
  - every texture is excluded by m_c/m_t.
- **KK modes and unification** (`cubic_shells`, `kk7_unification_shift`):
  - the cubic shells hold 6, 12, 8 states;
  - each PS-charged KK state shifts the SU(4)–SU(2) running by 19/3 (b-units);
  - with the compactification scale at 0.9Λ (6 states), Δα⁻¹ = 0.64; at 0.7Λ (18 states), Δα⁻¹ = 2.28. Either destroys the doc 55/60 unification fit;
  - with M_c ≥ Λ the tower decouples and changes nothing.

  - **Verdict:** numerology.
- **Seven lampstands.**
  - **ℤ₇ / T₇ = ℤ₇⋊ℤ₃** is in §1: parameter-free textures are degenerate, free ones are fitted.
  - **Fano plane / octonions** (`fano_cubic`): an antisymmetric Yukawa \(Y_{ij}=c_{ijk}v_k\) from the octonion structure constants gives singular values (1,1,1,1,1,1,0) for any v: three degenerate pairs plus one zero. Excluded.
  - **G₂-holonomy manifolds:** no explicit metric is known, so this is not testable here.
