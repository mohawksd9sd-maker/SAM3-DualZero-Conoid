# Mathematical Note XIII — Explicit $A_5$ / Bridge Intertwiner

**Date:** August 2026  
**Purpose:** Construct the unitary intertwiner between angular bridge modes and the $A_5$ permutation module on 12 vertices, completing the pure-math residual of Note VIII L2.

---

## 1. Two Hilbert spaces

**Geometric angular space.**  
On $S^1=\mathbb{R}/2\pi\mathbb{Z}$ with the twelve bridge points

$$
\theta_j = \frac{2\pi j}{12},\qquad j=0,\ldots,11,
$$

define the discrete bridge space

$$
\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{br}} := \mathbb{C}^{12} = \mathrm{span}\{\lvert j\rangle : j=0,\ldots,11\},
$$

with orthonormal basis $\langle j\mid k\rangle=\delta_{jk}$.

**Permutation module.**  
Identify $\{\lvert j\rangle\}$ with the twelve vertices of the icosahedron. The alternating group $A_5\cong \mathrm{Rot}(\mathrm{icosahedron})$ acts by permuting vertices:

$$
(\pi\cdot \psi)(j) = \psi(\pi^{-1}j),\qquad \pi\in A_5.
$$

This is the **permutation representation** $\mathrm{Perm}_{12}$.

## 2. Standard decomposition (classical)

**Theorem E (permutation representation of $A_5$ on 12 vertices).**  
As an $A_5$-representation,

$$
\mathrm{Perm}_{12} \cong 1 \oplus 3 \oplus 3' \oplus 5.
$$

**Proof.** Character computation (standard): $\chi_{\mathrm{Perm}}(g)=\#\mathrm{Fix}(g)$. Compare to the character table of $A_5$ (irreps $1,3,3',4,5$). The multiplicity of each irrep is $\langle \chi_{\mathrm{Perm}},\chi_\rho\rangle$; one finds $m_1=1$, $m_3=m_{3'}=1$, $m_5=1$, $m_4=0$. See e.g. any character table of $A_5$ / icosahedral group.

## 3. Fourier / bridge map

**Definition (bridge sampling).**  
For $\psi\in L^2(S^1)$, define the sampling operator $S:\,L^2(S^1)\to\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{br}}$ by

$$
(S\psi)_j := \frac{1}{\sqrt{12}}\sum_{k\in\mathbb{Z}} \hat\psi(k)\,e^{ik\theta_j}
\quad\text{(equivalently point evaluation on a dense band-limited subspace).}
$$

On the band-limited subspace

$$
B := \mathrm{span}\{e^{imv}: m=-5,\ldots,6\}\subset L^2(S^1)
$$

(12-dimensional), $S|_B: B\to\mathcal{H}_{\mathrm{br}}$ is unitary up to normalisation of the Fourier basis (DFT at 12 roots of unity).

**Proposition F (intertwining on band-limited sector).**  
The natural $C_{12}$ rotation $v\mapsto v+2\pi/12$ generates a cyclic action on $B$ matching the cyclic rotation of bridges. Extending to the full $A_5$ action on vertices defines a representation $\rho_{\mathrm{geo}}$ on $B$ such that $S$ intertwines $\rho_{\mathrm{geo}}$ with $\mathrm{Perm}_{12}$:

$$
S\,\rho_{\mathrm{geo}}(\pi) = \pi\, S\qquad(\pi\in A_5),
$$

for the standard identification of a 2-generator presentation of $A_5$ with icosahedral rotations (order-2 and order-3 or order-5 generators acting on vertices).

**Proof sketch.**  
(1) Cyclic rotation by $2\pi/12$ is the DFT shift — intertwines by construction of $S$.  
(2) $A_5$ is generated by the cyclic rotation of order 5 about a vertex axis together with an order-2 edge rotation (standard icosahedral generators).  
(3) Both generators permute the 12 vertices; transporting the action to band-limited functions via $S^{-1}$ yields $\rho_{\mathrm{geo}}$.  
(4) Unitarity of $S$ on $B$ gives a unitary intertwiner $B\cong \mathrm{Perm}_{12}$.

**Corollary F′.**  
Under $S$,

$$
B \cong 1\oplus 3\oplus 3'\oplus 5
$$

as $A_5$-modules. Projectors onto isotypes are the standard character projectors

$$
P_\rho = \frac{\dim\rho}{|A_5|}\sum_{g\in A_5}\chi_\rho(g^{-1})\,\rho_{\mathrm{geo}}(g).
$$

## 4. Dirac equivariance hypothesis

**Hypothesis H_eq.**  
The full Dirac operator on the conoid (radial $\times$ angular) is reduced so that its angular part preserves the band-limited sector $B$ modulo tip-localised errors that vanish in the continuum defect limit, and so that $D$ commutes with $\rho_{\mathrm{geo}}$ up to those errors.

Under H_eq, near-zero modes may be chosen as $A_5$-isotype eigenmodes via $P_\rho$.

**Status.** H_eq is the precise structure hypothesis linking geometry to representation theory. On a pure angular Dirac on $S^1$ it holds exactly for Fourier modes. On the full $(u,v)$ operator with locked $f(u,v)$, equivariance under the discrete bridge group is imposed as the residual symmetry of SAM3; proving $[D,\rho_{\mathrm{geo}}(g)]=0$ exactly for all $g\in A_5$ on the nonlinear metric is equivalent to showing the metric and spin connection are invariant under the corresponding isometries — which holds for the **angular lattice symmetry** (cyclic $C_{12}$) exactly, and for the full $A_5$ as the discrete residual of the model’s bridge construction (structure equation of the programme).

## 5. Three light generations from isotypes

Not all summands in $1\oplus 3\oplus 3'\oplus 5$ remain light: the tip Casimir / radial potential (Hypothesis H_tip, Note XII) lifts some isotypes. The locked light assignment uses three sectors with weights $C_g=(6/5,1,4/5)$, corresponding to a choice of three light radial channels in the equivariant decomposition — not to keeping the entire 12-dimensional permutation module light.

**Proposition G (counting).**  
Under H_eq + H_tip with exactly three light Casimir channels, the continuum chiral near-zero space is three-dimensional as an isotype-sum of light channels.
