# Mathematical Note XI — Naturalness of Intermediate Constructions

**Date:** August 2026  
**Purpose:** Address the charge that CKM angles, Higgs class, and lepton bands rest on intermediate objects (defect operators, Casimir weights, overlaps) whose naturalness is unclear.  
**Rule:** Separate **forced by geometry** from **convenient modelling**.

---

## 1. Continuum defect locus and width

**Construction.** Defect overlaps $\eta_{ij}$ are computed from continuum wavefunctions against a tip defect with

- locus = tip curvature maximum,  
- width $= a\Delta\theta$ with $\Delta\theta=2\pi/12$.

**Naturalness argument.**

1. Zero-mode and near-zero density for the Dirac operator concentrates where the metric scale $f$ is smallest / curvature peaks — the tip.  
2. The only angular scale already in the geometry is the bridge spacing $\Delta\theta$. Taking defect width $\propto\Delta\theta$ introduces **no new continuous parameter**.  
3. Overlaps $\eta_{ij}=\langle\psi_i|P_{\mathrm{def}}|\psi_j\rangle$ are then standard quantum-mechanical matrix elements, not fitted Yukawas.

**Verdict:** Forced at the level of “localise where the geometry demands; normalise width to the only angular scale.”  
**Residual:** The $O(1)$ factor in width $= a\Delta\theta$ is discrete-conventional; heat-kernel tip split (doc 32) reduces dependence on ad hoc window shapes.

## 2. Casimir generation weights $C_g=(6/5,1,4/5)$

**Construction.** Radial tip potentials weighted by Casimir eigenvalues on three generation sectors.

**Naturalness argument.**

1. Once $A_5$/2I acts on the internal Hilbert space, quadratic Casimir is the **canonical** invariant Laplacian eigenvalue labelling isotypes.  
2. Using Casimir as a radial mass-ordering parameter is the same logic as “order by representation label,” not a PDG fit.  
3. The specific triple $(6/5,1,4/5)$ is the locked module assignment in the repository; it must match the 2I-module decomposition used for generations (Note VIII L2–L3).

**Verdict:** *Form* of Casimir weighting is natural; *which* three eigenvalues appear is model-module data (audit against the explicit 2I action).  
**Residual:** Independent recomputation of the module Casimirs from an explicit intertwiner.

## 3. Cabibbo $\theta_{12}\approx\eta_{12}\times\pi/12$

**Construction.** Product of continuum overlap $\eta_{12}$ and bridge angle unit $\pi/12$.

**Naturalness argument.**

1. $\pi/12=\Delta\theta/2$ is pure discrete geometry (12 bridges).  
2. $\eta_{12}$ is a continuum matrix element (Section 1).  
3. No continuous CKM parameter is inserted by hand.

**Verdict:** Natural as “overlap × atomic angle.”  
**Residual:** Why the map is linear $\eta\times\pi/12$ rather than $\arcsin\eta\cdot\pi/12$ etc. is a modelling choice at the present precision; both agree at leading order when $\eta\sim O(1)$.

---

## 4. CP phase $\phi=2\pi/5$

**Construction.** Golden-ratio / pentagonal angle from 2I ($e^{2\pi i/5}$ in $C_5\subset 2I$).

**Naturalness argument.**  
$2\pi/5$ is the minimal positive rotation angle appearing in icosahedral / binary-icosahedral geometry. Using it as the module CP phase is canonical once 2I is chosen — not a fitted $\delta_{\mathrm{CKM}}$.

**Verdict:** Natural given 2I.  
**Residual:** Precise embedding into the Yukawa bi-unitary phase convention (quark vs lepton).

---

## 5. Higgs as $a_4$ class

**Construction.** Higgs mass in 124–127 GeV band from Seeley $a_4$, not Dual-Zero digit tuning.

**Naturalness argument.**  
In spectral action, the Higgs potential **always** sits in $a_4$. Quoting a class/band rather than $125.10$ GeV is the natural claim level given scheme residuals (Note IX).

**Verdict:** Natural claim discipline.  
**Residual:** Sub-GeV sharpness.

## 6. Lepton bands

**Construction.** Large $\delta_{\mathrm{PMNS}}$ from same $\phi$; NH from $C_g$ ordering; $m_{\beta\beta}$ from standard seesaw bookkeeping with free Majorana phases.

**Naturalness argument.**  
Sharing $\phi$ across quarks and leptons is the **least** arbitrary choice once module CP is geometric. Leaving Majorana phases unfrozen is honest, not a hidden fit.

**Verdict:** Natural structure; absolute eV scales still use external $\Delta m^2$ as unit inputs (Note VI).
