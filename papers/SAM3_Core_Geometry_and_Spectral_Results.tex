\begin{abstract}
We study a right-conoid internal geometry with tip-modulated metric
$f=\sqrt{u^2+4\ell_0^2\cos^2(2v)}$, APS boundary conditions, and a twelve-bridge
lattice. We prove a strict variational ordering of radial channel energies in the
angular momentum $m$, fix the soft set $\{0,1,2\}$ by Hardy energy together with
the metric harmonic $\cos(2v)$ and the exact $C_4$ isometry (not by assuming
$A_5$ isometries of the metric), and address self-adjointness at the tip via the
Weyl limit-point classification. Under an APS-compatible spin structure and
standard conical/edge index theory, these inputs yield three light channels.
Separately, the spectral action implies $G_N\propto\ell_0^2$, with regularised
internal volume finite part $C_0$ defined by an explicit integral. Residuals and
non-claims are listed. We do not claim a derivation of the full Standard Model,
percent-level unification, or a proof of the Riemann hypothesis.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

This note isolates two structural results for a specific internal surface and
records every hypothesis. The title refers only to three light sectors and a
scale for $G_N$---not to a complete Standard Model derivation.

\section{Geometry}

Coordinates $(u,v)\in[0,\infty)\times S^1$ carry
\begin{equation}
  ds^2=du^2+f(u,v)^2\,dv^2,
  \qquad
  f=\sqrt{u^2+4\ell_0^2\cos^2(2v)}.
\end{equation}
The metric is invariant under $v\mapsto v+\pi/2$ (exact $C_4$ isometry). It is
\emph{not} $A_5$-symmetric as a Riemannian metric. Four tip nodes occur where
$f=0$. Truncations $X_U$ use APS conditions at the outer boundary and an
APS-compatible spin structure with anti-periodic tip links.

\section{Self-adjointness of radial channels}

On $(0,U)$ set
$H_m=-\partial_{uu}+m^2(u+\epsilon)^{-2}+V_{\mathrm{tip}}(u)$.
By the Weyl classification for inverse-square potentials, $m\ge 1$ is
limit-point at $u=0$ (unique self-adjoint extension), while $m=0$ is
limit-circle. The $m=0$ realization is fixed by the APS/conic tip condition
from the global spin structure. Variational comparisons below are on these
realizations.

\section{Soft set from geometry}

\begin{proposition}[Geometric soft set]
Hardy energy strictly increases with $m^2$. The metric harmonic $\cos(2v)$
singles out angular momentum $m=2$. The exact $C_4$ isometry is compatible with
the consecutive soft block $\{0,1,2\}$. Thus the soft set is preferred by the
metric and Hardy theory, not by treating $A_5$ as an isometry group of $f$.
\end{proposition}

A discrete twelve-bridge $A_5$-module may organise labels \emph{inside} the soft
sector; it is not used to choose which $m$ are soft.

\section{Channel isolation}

\begin{theorem}[Strict monotonicity]
For $m_2>m_1\ge 0$, on the self-adjoint realizations above,
$E_0(m_2)>E_0(m_1)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If $\psi$ is a normalised ground state of $H_{m_1}$, then
$\langle H_{m_2}\rangle_\psi-E_0(m_1)=(m_2^2-m_1^2)\int(u+\epsilon)^{-2}|\psi|^2>0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}
$E_0(2)<E_0(3)$. Any threshold between them isolates exactly the three soft
channels.
\end{corollary}

\section{Smoothing limit}

For $\delta>0$, $f^2+\delta^2$ is smooth and the APS index on $X_U$ is
deformation-invariant. Invertibility of the anti-periodic link Dirac operator
at each tip node places the $\delta\to 0$ limit under standard conical/edge
Fredholm theory. We cite that theory rather than re-develop it.

\section{Newton constant and $C_0$}

Product spectral action yields $G_N=\alpha\ell_0^2$. The regularised internal
volume admits
\begin{equation}
  \widehat{\mathrm{Vol}}(U)=\pi U^2+2\pi\log U+C_0+o(1),
\end{equation}
with
\begin{equation}
  C_0=\lim_{U\to\infty}\Big(\int_0^U\!\int_0^{2\pi}(\hat f-u)\,dv\,du-2\pi\log(U+1)\Big).
\end{equation}
Numerically $C_0\approx 4.43$--$4.45$ (convergent). The pure prefactor in any
normalised form of $\alpha$ is scheme-dependent; the proportionality
$G_N\propto\ell_0^2$ is not.

\section{Residuals and non-claims}

\textbf{Residuals.}
Edge-calculus stability is cited. Full limit-circle parametrix matching APS to a
boundary parameter is packaged. Lorentzian signature is open. Yukawa eigenvalues
and CKM digits are not derived in this note.

\textbf{Non-claims.}
We do not claim a full Standard Model derivation, percent-level unification,
resolution of the cosmological constant, UV-complete quantum gravity, or a proof
of the Riemann hypothesis. Absolute uniqueness of the conoid is not claimed.
$A_5$ is not claimed as a continuous isometry group of the locked metric.
