\begin{abstract}
We study a right-conoid internal geometry with twelve binary-icosahedral bridges
and Atiyah--Patodi--Singer boundary conditions. Under an APS-compatible spin
structure and standard conical/edge index theory, the continuum Dirac operator
supports exactly three light chiral sectors after angular-momentum barrier
isolation. Separately, the spectral action on the product geometry yields
Newton's constant proportional to the square of the internal scale. We state all
hypotheses, prove the channel isolation inequalities, package the tip singularity
via existing edge-calculus results, and list residuals explicitly. No claim is
made of a complete theory of everything, gauge unification to high precision, or
a proof of the Riemann hypothesis.
\end{abstract}

\section{Introduction}

Almost-commutative spectral triples realise the Standard Model gauge sector from
a finite algebra combined with a compact Riemannian spin geometry
\cite{CCM}. Extending the internal geometry beyond a finite space raises the
possibility of geometric generation structure and a gravitational scale fixed by
Seeley--DeWitt coefficients.

This note isolates two results for a specific internal surface---a right conoid
with tip modulation and a twelve-point bridge lattice related to the binary
icosahedral group---and records every hypothesis required.

\section{Geometric setup}

Let coordinates $(u,v)\in[0,\infty)\times S^1$ carry the metric
\begin{equation}
  ds^2 = du^2 + f(u,v)^2\,dv^2,
  \qquad
  f(u,v)=\sqrt{u^2+4\ell_0^2\cos^2(2v)}.
\end{equation}
The function $f$ vanishes only at four tip nodes
$(u,v)=(0,\pi/4+k\pi/2)$. The continuous isometry group contains a cyclic group
$C_4$ acting by $v\mapsto v+\pi/2$; it does \emph{not} contain the full alternating
group $A_5$ as metric isometries. An $A_5$-module structure is carried instead by
the discrete twelve-bridge sampling space
$\mathbb{C}^{12}\cong 1\oplus 3\oplus 3'\oplus 5$.

Truncations $X_U=[0,U]\times S^1$ are equipped with APS boundary conditions at
the outer boundary and an APS-compatible spin structure that restricts to the
anti-periodic structure on tip links.

\section{Channel isolation}

Angular Fourier modes on the bridge circle are labelled by effective angular
momentum $m\in\mathbb{Z}_{\ge 0}$. The representation content of the light block
$1\oplus 3\oplus 3'$ is supported on soft modes $m\in\{0,1,2\}$; the five-dimensional
summand is supported on heavy modes $m\ge 3$ (dictionary in the repository notes).

\begin{theorem}[Strict monotonicity]\label{thm:mono}
On $(0,U)$ with Dirichlet or APS-compatible ends, let
$H_m=-\partial_{uu}+m^2(u+\epsilon)^{-2}+V_{\mathrm{tip}}(u)$ with $V_{\mathrm{tip}}$
independent of $m$. Then $m_2>m_1\ge 0$ implies $E_0(m_2)>E_0(m_1)$.
\end{theorem}

\begin{proof}
If $\psi$ is a normalised ground state of $H_{m_1}$, then
$\langle H_{m_2}\rangle_\psi-E_0(m_1)=(m_2^2-m_1^2)\int (u+\epsilon)^{-2}|\psi|^2>0$.
\end{proof}

\begin{corollary}[Three-plus-zero isolation]\label{cor:iso}
$E_0(2)<E_0(3)$. For any threshold $\tau$ between them, exactly the three soft
channels lie below $\tau$ and no heavy channel does.
\end{corollary}

\section{Smoothing limit at the tip}

For $\delta>0$ the smoothed metric $f^2+\delta^2$ is non-degenerate and the APS index
on $X_U$ is deformation-invariant. The four tip nodes are quadratic cones; their
link Dirac operators are invertible under the anti-periodic spin structure. By
standard conical/edge Fredholm theory\cite{MelroseAPS,APS1975}, the
$\delta\to 0$ limit of the APS index exists and equals the edge index of the singular
metric. Details and a referee checklist appear in the accompanying notes.

\section{Newton constant}

On a product spectral triple the Seeley--DeWitt coefficient $a_2$ produces the
Einstein--Hilbert term. With internal scale $\ell_0$,
\begin{equation}
  G_N = \alpha\,\ell_0^2
\end{equation}
for a dimensionless $\alpha$ fixed by cutoff moments and the regularised internal
volume finite part $C_0\approx 4.45$. The proportionality is structural; the pure
numerical prefactor used in the repository is convention-locked and scheme-dependent.

\section{Residuals and non-claims}

\textbf{Residuals.}
(1)~Edge-calculus stability is cited rather than re-proved.
(2)~The soft/heavy dictionary is representation-theoretic input.
(3)~Unreduced two-dimensional Dirac mixing is controlled only at leading selection-rule level.
(4)~Production channel numerics illustrate $\Delta(U)$ but are not used as a substitute for Theorem~\ref{thm:mono}.

\textbf{Non-claims.}
We do not claim a complete Theory of Everything, percent-level gauge unification,
a resolution of the cosmological constant problem, ultraviolet-complete quantum
gravity, or a proof of the Riemann hypothesis. Absolute uniqueness of the conoid
among all internal geometries is not claimed.

\section{Conclusion}

Under the stated hypotheses, the internal Dirac spectrum isolates three light
sectors by a strict variational inequality, and the spectral action fixes
$G_N\propto\ell_0^2$. Both statements are designed for external audit.
