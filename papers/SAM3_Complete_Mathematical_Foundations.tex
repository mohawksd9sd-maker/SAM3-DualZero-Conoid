\section{Right Conoid Geometry (LOCKED)}

Parametrization:
\[
\mathbf{r}(u,v) = (u \cos v,\ u \sin v,\ \ell_0 \sin(2v)).
\]
Induced metric (direct computation):
\[
|\mathbf{r}_u|^2 = 1,\qquad
|\mathbf{r}_v|^2 = u^2 + 4\ell_0^2\cos^2(2v),
\]
hence
\[
ds^2 = du^2 + f(u,v)^2 dv^2, \quad
f(u,v) = \sqrt{u^2 + 4\ell_0^2 \cos^2(2v)}.
\]
\textbf{Coefficient is 4, not 16.} (The factor 16 was an error: $r_v$ has
$2\ell_0\cos(2v)$ in the $z$-component, so $4\ell_0^2\cos^2(2v)$ after squaring.)

Gaussian curvature on the non-degenerate locus:
\[
K = -\frac{4\ell_0^2 \cos^2(2v)}{\bigl(u^2 + 4\ell_0^2 \cos^2(2v)\bigr)^2}.
\]
