\begin{abstract}
We outline geometric ingredients in SAM3 that motivate a variational selection principle for the critical line: Dual-Zero regularization, graded structure, bridge remainders, and an information action $S_I$. Stationarity of $S_I$ selects configurations with $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$ internal to the model. This is not a proof that every non-trivial zero of the classical Riemann zeta function lies on the critical line.
\end{abstract}

\section{What is proposed}
\begin{itemize}

\item A variational selection principle: critical points of $S_I$ satisfy $\operatorname{Re}(s)=1/2$.
\end{itemize}

\section{What is not claimed}
